# REL-101 · Transfer eligibility and preapproval

North Alder Instruments | Effective January 1, 2026

## REL-101.1 Who qualifies

The relocation benefit is available to an existing employee whom North Alder Instruments asks to move their primary residence for a permanent transfer between two company offices in the United States. The receiving manager must identify the new office in a written transfer notice. An employee who changes homes to shorten an existing commute, takes a temporary assignment, or chooses to work remotely from another state does not qualify. New hires should consult their offer letter for any moving arrangements.

A transfer notice establishes the business reason for the move. It does not authorize spending. Before paying a deposit, booking travel, or asking a mover to start work, obtain the written move authorization described below. A manager can discuss likely costs with you while you prepare the request. That conversation alone does not make those costs eligible for reimbursement.

## REL-101.2 Requesting authorization

Send People Operations a copy of the transfer notice, the names of the sending and receiving offices, your planned move dates, and an estimate for each expense category you expect to use. Identify anyone in your household who will travel with you. For this benefit, a household member is someone who shares your primary residence before the transfer and will share it after the transfer. Include vendor estimates where available so the receiving manager can assess the budget.

People Operations checks eligibility and forwards the proposed budget to the receiving manager. If the manager approves the budget, People Operations issues a written move authorization containing a transfer reference, the approved categories, and a maximum amount for each category. Keep that authorization with your move records. If the request is returned for clarification, send the missing information to People Operations using the same transfer reference if one has already been assigned.

## REL-101.3 Changes before spending

Check the authorization against your plans before making reservations. If you need to add a category or increase an approved amount, request a written amendment from People Operations before committing to the additional cost. Explain what changed and attach a revised estimate. The receiving manager reviews the revised budget and People Operations sends the decision to you. The original authorization continues to govern until a written amendment is issued.

For example, an authorization for a household shipment does not cover a hotel booking just because the mover's quote came in under budget. Request temporary lodging as a separate category before booking it. The limits and exclusions in REL-102 apply to every authorization, including amendments. A lower approved budget can limit reimbursement even when a price falls within the company limit.

## REL-105.1 Requesting cash before departure

An employee with a written move authorization may ask Treasury for a single advance against employee-paid final travel and temporary lodging. Submit form ADV-4 with the authorization and expected payment dates at least five business days before the first purchase. Treasury calculates half of the combined approved employee-paid travel and lodging estimates, subject to a maximum advance of $800. Exclude any portion already booked on a company account. Moving-company orders and lease release fees cannot be advanced.
