# REL-102 · Covered relocation expenses

North Alder Instruments | Effective January 1, 2026

## REL-102.1 Applying the limits

Use the categories listed in your written move authorization to plan purchases. For each category, North Alder Instruments reimburses the eligible amount you actually paid, up to the lower of the policy limit and the authorized budget for that category. Apply any refund or vendor discount before claiming the expense. Do not transfer a balance between categories. REL-101 explains how to request a change before you incur additional costs.

## REL-102.2 Household shipment

The shipment category covers packing supplies, packing labor, transportation of household goods, and unloading at the new residence. The policy limit is $5,000 per transfer, including vendor fees and taxes. You may hire a mover or rent a truck for a self-managed shipment. For a rental, the rental charge, fuel for the truck, and packing supplies share the same shipment limit. List fuel for the rental truck with shipment costs to keep it separate from personal vehicle travel.

Personal storage, shipment of a second motor vehicle, and the purchase of furniture for the new residence are excluded. If a mover includes storage and delivery on a single invoice, ask for an itemized breakdown. You can claim the eligible delivery portion even though you used the same vendor for storage. Record only the amount due for the covered services after any discount.

## REL-102.3 Final travel to the destination

Final travel covers one direct, one-way journey from the old residence to the destination for you and the household members identified in the authorization. Eligible costs include economy airfare, standard checked baggage fees, and ground transportation to or from the airport. For travel by personal vehicle, claim actual fuel and toll costs for the direct route. The combined final travel limit is $1,200 per transfer for the whole traveling household.

A personal detour or an upgrade to a premium cabin is at your own expense. If you choose a premium fare, keep a quote for economy travel on the same itinerary obtained when you book. Finance can use that quote to determine the eligible portion within your authorized travel amount. Preliminary house-hunting trips and return trips to collect belongings are excluded.

## REL-102.4 Temporary lodging

Temporary lodging at the destination is covered for up to ten nights while you arrange occupancy of your new residence. The room charge and mandatory lodging taxes together are eligible up to $150 per night for the household. Apply the limit separately to each night. An unused amount from one night cannot offset a higher charge on another night. Meals, parking, laundry, and minibar charges are excluded from the lodging calculation.

The following two-night folio shows how Finance applies the limit before checking the authorized lodging budget. The room and tax column includes mandatory lodging taxes. The parking column shows a separate charge on the same bill.

| Stay | Room and tax | Parking | Amount eligible |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First night | $130 | $15 | $130 |
| Second night | $170 | $20 | $150 |
| Total | $300 | $35 | $280 |

Request a hotel folio showing each night's charges so Finance can verify this calculation. A booking confirmation alone shows the reservation price and may omit taxes or later adjustments.

## REL-102.5 Costs paid directly by the company

If the company pays a vendor directly, identify the service as company-paid on the claim and exclude it from the reimbursement total. Direct payments count toward the applicable category budget and policy limit. For example, ask People Operations how much of the shipment budget remains after a company-paid mover invoice before buying additional packing supplies. Follow REL-103 for the records to submit for expenses you paid yourself.

## REL-102.6 Charges from the old landlord

Lease termination is a separate, discretionary category governed by REL-106. An authorization for shipment, final travel or temporary lodging cannot be used to settle an old lease. Ordinary rent on either residence remains the employee's responsibility even when the two lease terms overlap. Ask the landlord to identify the contractual release fee separately from rent, damage and deposit adjustments.

## REL-106.1 Approval before signing a release

A renter who must vacate the old primary residence before the lease ends may request lease termination on the written move authorization. Send People Operations the signed lease, its early-release provision and the landlord's proposed settlement before signing the release. The receiving manager approves a category budget. Where the lease has no release clause, a written negotiated buyout can serve as the proposed settlement if it releases the employee from future rent. An email acknowledging receipt of keys is insufficient evidence of that release.

## REL-106.2 Net fee and maximum

The eligible lease release amount is the contractual buyout less any deposit or concession the landlord applies to that fee. Reimbursement is limited to the smallest of the net fee, one month's base rent, $1,800 and the approved lease termination budget. Use base rent before utilities, parking and amenity charges. The deposit itself is ineligible. If the landlord returns the deposit to the employee instead of applying it to the buyout, there is no deposit credit against the fee.

## REL-106.3 Reading a landlord settlement

A landlord may put cleaning, repainting, unpaid utilities, rent through the handover date and the release fee on one statement. Only the release fee enters the calculation. Provide the signed release and an itemized closing statement showing how the deposit was applied, together with proof of the employee's payment. If the landlord later reduces the fee because a replacement tenant moved in early, send the revised settlement to Finance using the claim confirmation number.

## REL-107.1 Split stays and overlapping rooms

The housing desk counts occupied destination nights across hotels, furnished units and extended-stay properties. Changing properties does not restart the ten-night allowance. If two household members occupy separate rooms on the same date, combine eligible room charges and mandatory taxes for that date before applying the household nightly limit in REL-102.4. A reservation made to hold a vacant room before anyone arrives is ineligible. Ask for folios that show the dates each room was actually used.

## REL-107.2 Weekly invoices for furnished units

For a single flat occupancy charge covering identified nights, divide the charge and its mandatory lodging tax evenly across the invoiced nights. Apply the nightly limit only to eligible occupied dates and then apply the authorized category budget. Remove refundable security deposits, separate housekeeping packages and key deposits before dividing. A furnished apartment invoice that lists a monthly amount without dates must be corrected by the operator before Finance can allocate it.

## REL-107.3 Hotel deposit and card holds

A pending card authorization at check-in is a hold. Wait for the final folio and posted payment before filing. If a prepaid deposit is credited on that folio, attach the deposit receipt and the balance receipt to the same stay. Claim the eligible stay once. Ask the housing desk to resolve an unreleased hold with the property. The hold cannot be listed as a second night's payment.

## REL-109.1 When an airline changes the booked route

Keep the original itinerary and the airline's replacement itinerary if a carrier cancels a direct flight during the move. An involuntary connection on the replacement route remains part of the same one-way journey. Attach the carrier's cancellation notice if the new ticket shows a different city or travel date. A replacement bought independently should identify the refund or credit due on the unused ticket. Finance needs the net cost borne by the employee across both tickets.

If the employee requests an extra stop to visit family, obtain a same-day comparison for the direct journey when booking. The eligible airfare is the lower of the amount paid and the comparable economy fare for the approved journey. If the airline provides a cash refund after settlement, report it on the original claim. Changing planes under an airline-issued itinerary and choosing a multi-day personal stop have different evidence requirements.

## REL-109.2 Using both a personal car and a rental truck

A household can drive one personal car and one rental truck on the same route. Keep the fuel slips for the two vehicles separate. Fuel for the loaded rental truck belongs to household shipment. Fuel and tolls for the household car belong to final travel. No cents-per-mile allowance is paid. The employee should mark which vehicle received each fuel purchase when both vehicles stop at the same station on the same day.

A rental agreement can include a deposit, a truck charge and an estimate for fuel. Use the closed rental invoice and the employee's actual fuel receipts. If the rental company charged to refill the tank, include that paid refill once in shipment. If the employee filled the tank before returning the truck and the company did not charge for fuel, submit only the pump receipt. Mileage printed on the agreement supports the journey, but multiplying the miles by a separate business travel rate would duplicate the vehicle cost.

## REL-109.3 Separating personal and office freight

Packing a personal desk in the household truck is eligible when it belonged to the employee at the old residence. Shipping an employer-owned standing desk is a Facilities expense. Ask the site coordinator about the asset listing if ownership is unclear. A replacement desk bought after arrival remains a furniture purchase, regardless of whether buying new cost less than transport. A mover's estimate for the old desk is not evidence of an amount actually spent.

For self-packed moves, an itemized store receipt should distinguish consumable materials such as cartons and tape from reusable purchases such as a hand truck or tool set. Consumable packing materials are eligible. Buying equipment for future use is personal. If an employee returns unopened boxes, retain the store credit slip and reduce the claimed materials cost by the return, even if the store credited a different card.

## REL-109.4 Freight held before delivery

A carrier may collect the household goods on one day and deliver several days later. A normal line-haul interval included in the agreed transportation price does not need to be valued as separate storage. A warehouse or container rental charge for holding goods at the employee's request is personal storage. If the invoice bundles the extra holding period with destination delivery, request the carrier's delivery-only amount and the amount attributable to the hold.

For a portable container, the supplier may charge for delivery of the empty unit, collection of the loaded unit, transport to the destination, unloading access and an ongoing rental. Eligible transport and ordinary loading or unloading services share the shipment limit. A monthly charge for keeping the unit after delivery is excluded. If the supplier cannot identify the part of a bundled charge attributable to eligible service, Finance can review the other documented lines while awaiting a revised invoice.

## REL-109.5 Overnight travel before reaching the new city

Destination lodging starts when an approved household member occupies temporary accommodation at the transfer destination. A motel halfway through a drive does not qualify as destination lodging. An employee taking a side trip cannot move those nights to the destination claim by using the same hotel chain. Record the property address as well as the dates on each folio. If a reservation service issues one receipt for two properties, obtain the property-level details from the operator.

A hotel near the new office also may be used for training before a permanent move. The employee should request a separate folio for the training dates under the business travel record. Once a household member takes up temporary housing as part of the authorized move, eligible nights are counted under REL-107.1. A manager's approval of the earlier business trip establishes neither a lodging budget for the household nor the date it took up residence.

## REL-109.6 Credits, vouchers and mixed tender

If a ticket or room was partly paid with loyalty points, record the actual employee-paid cash portion and any mandatory tax paid in cash. The employee cannot claim the retail value of redeemed points. When a corporate booking agent applies a company travel credit, identify the credit as company-funded and provide the receipt for any balance the employee paid. Finance uses the funding breakdown to determine the amount payable to the employee.

For example, a hotel applies a promotional discount before collecting a deposit and the remaining balance. The discounted room and tax amount is the starting point for the nightly calculation. The deposit and balance describe how that amount was paid. Adding either payment on top of the discounted folio total would count part of the stay twice. If the balance receipt also includes parking, mark the excluded charge on the final folio.

## REL-109.7 Property purchase and household setup

The domestic transfer program has no category for buying or selling a residence. Brokerage commissions, inspection fees, mortgage points and the cost of staging a home are personal. The lease release provision covers an approved cost of leaving the old rented home. It does not cover an application fee, security deposit or first month's rent on the replacement home. A request from a landlord to prepay rent before handing over keys does not change the nature of the charge.

Connection fees for internet or utilities and purchases of curtains, appliances or household cleaning supplies are also personal. When they appear on the same retailer receipt as packing tape or boxes, identify the covered lines and their share of sales tax. If the retailer offers a discount against the entire basket without allocating it by item, allocate the discount in proportion to each item's pre-discount price before computing the eligible portion.
