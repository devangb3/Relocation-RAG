# REL-101 · Transfer eligibility and preapproval

North Alder Instruments | Effective January 1, 2026

## REL-101.1 Who qualifies

The relocation benefit is available to an existing employee whom North Alder Instruments asks to move their primary residence for a permanent transfer between two company offices in the United States. The receiving manager must identify the new office in a written transfer notice. An employee who changes homes to shorten an existing commute, takes a temporary assignment, or chooses to work remotely from another state does not qualify. New hires should consult their offer letter for any moving arrangements.

A transfer notice establishes the business reason for the move. It does not authorize spending. Before paying a deposit, booking travel, or asking a mover to start work, obtain the written move authorization described below. A manager can discuss likely costs with you while you prepare the request. That conversation alone does not make those costs eligible for reimbursement.

## REL-101.2 Requesting authorization

Send People Operations a copy of the transfer notice, the names of the sending and receiving offices, your planned move dates, and an estimate for each expense category you expect to use. Identify anyone in your household who will travel with you. For this benefit, a household member is someone who shares your primary residence before the transfer and will share it after the transfer. Include vendor estimates where available so the receiving manager can assess the budget.

People Operations checks eligibility and forwards the proposed budget to the receiving manager. If the manager approves the budget, People Operations issues a written move authorization containing a transfer reference, the approved categories, and a maximum amount for each category. Keep that authorization with your move records. If the request is returned for clarification, send the missing information to People Operations using the same transfer reference if one has already been assigned.

## REL-101.3 Changes before spending

Check the authorization against your plans before making reservations. If you need to add a category or increase an approved amount, request a written amendment from People Operations before committing to the additional cost. Explain what changed and attach a revised estimate. The receiving manager reviews the revised budget and People Operations sends the decision to you. The original authorization continues to govern until a written amendment is issued.

For example, an authorization for a household shipment does not cover a hotel booking just because the mover's quote came in under budget. Request temporary lodging as a separate category before booking it. The limits and exclusions in REL-102 apply to every authorization, including amendments. A lower approved budget can limit reimbursement even when a price falls within the company limit.

## REL-101.4 Closing the move record

After the move, tell People Operations the date you arrived at the destination to take up residence. People Operations records that date as the move completion date. The date your boxes are delivered or the date you first report to the new office may differ. If the recorded date is incorrect, contact People Operations with your travel or occupancy record so the move record can be corrected.

Use the transfer reference on correspondence about the move and when filing your claim. Retain the authorization and any written amendments. REL-102 explains which costs to list, and REL-103 explains how to document them and when to file.

## REL-101.5 Two employees in one household

When two employees move from the same residence to the same destination, People Operations links their transfer references. The employees designate one reference for shared expenses before booking. List the mover, the hotel room and any shared car on that reference. Each employee may submit a separately purchased ticket under their own authorization. Finance checks the linked records for duplicate charges. A second transfer notice does not give the household a second limit for a shared service.

## REL-104.1 Comparing written estimates

For a professional mover estimated at $2,500 or more, send Purchasing two itemized estimates before selecting the carrier. Compare the same pickup address, delivery address, inventory and requested dates. A telephone estimate can be used to plan a budget, but Purchasing needs the written offers to place an order. If only one carrier serves the route, Purchasing may sign a single-source note documenting the carriers contacted. Attach the note to the move authorization. The quote requirement applies to professional carriers. An employee renting a truck uses the self-managed shipment rules in REL-102.2.

## REL-104.2 Purchase order and payment

Purchasing issues a move order number after People Operations releases the authorization. Give that number to the carrier for direct billing. The estimate reserves money on the order. Once Accounts Payable posts the carrier's final invoice, the posted charge replaces the estimate. Use that charge when working out the shipment budget left for employee purchases. If the invoice is pending, ask Purchasing to confirm the balance before buying more supplies.

## REL-104.3 A second truck at the delivery address

A carrier may call a transfer to a smaller truck a shuttle, access charge or last-mile fee. A shuttle is eligible transportation when the destination cannot accommodate the line-haul truck. Send Purchasing the carrier's written explanation and price before accepting the service. Purchasing adds it to the move order if the shipment authorization has room. If the new total exceeds the authorization, People Operations must first approve an amendment under REL-101.3. A surcharge for an employee's preferred delivery hour is a personal scheduling charge.

## REL-104.4 Missing carton or broken goods

Compare the numbered inventory with the cartons at delivery. Mark a missing carton or visible damage on the delivery record and keep a copy signed by the driver. Send the inventory number, photographs and order number to Purchasing, which opens a carrier service case. Track replacement or repair through that case. Do not enter the estimated value of the goods as a packing or transportation expense. If a carrier credits a transportation charge, Purchasing reconciles a direct bill. For an employee-paid charge, report the credit on the original claim.

## REL-105.1 Requesting cash before departure

An employee with a written move authorization may ask Treasury for a single advance against employee-paid final travel and temporary lodging. Submit form ADV-4 with the authorization and expected payment dates at least five business days before the first purchase. Treasury calculates half of the combined approved employee-paid travel and lodging estimates, subject to a maximum advance of $800. Exclude any portion already booked on a company account. Moving-company orders and lease release fees cannot be advanced.

## REL-105.2 Recording the deposit

Treasury assigns the deposit a settlement ID beginning with AV. Check that it appears in the relocation claim under Advances received. Enter eligible purchases at their full employee-paid amounts. Reducing each receipt by part of the advance as well would deduct the deposit twice. If the portal shows an approved advance that never reached your account, contact Treasury with the settlement ID before confirming the balance.

## REL-105.3 Closing an outstanding advance

Finance first applies the expense rules and category budgets, then deducts the advance from the total approved employee-paid expenses on the transfer. A positive balance is payable to the employee. For a negative balance, Treasury issues a return notice for the excess. Repay within ten business days of that notice using its settlement ID and the payment instructions on the notice. The date of a hotel checkout does not start the repayment clock. Keep the Treasury acknowledgement with the claim.

## REL-108.1 Converting a temporary assignment to a permanent transfer

When an employee takes a temporary assignment, the sending office continues to own the travel record until the receiving office issues a permanent transfer notice. People Operations opens the relocation record on receipt of that notice. The first day worked at the temporary location may be months earlier. Keep the assignment orders with the transfer notice so Finance can see where one arrangement ends and the other begins.

For example, a technician works on an installation for six weeks, renting a room near the receiving office. The site manager then offers a permanent transfer. The technician accepts, receives move authorization and brings the household to the new city the following month. The earlier installation stay remains on the project travel record. The technician may request destination lodging for the authorized move. If the property issues one bill spanning both stays, obtain a date-by-date folio and mark the changeover. A change in job status does not make earlier hotel charges available to claim again.

## REL-108.2 Households traveling on different dates

An employee may leave ahead of a spouse, partner or other approved household member. At authorization, identify each anticipated one-way trip and the reason for separate departure dates. The receiving manager reviews the combined cost against one household travel budget. People Operations records each traveler on the same transfer reference. If someone on the original roster decides to remain behind permanently, tell People Operations before using their reservation. Ask the booking agent to identify the ticket that can be refunded or credited.

Separate departure dates can also affect the move completion entry. Establish when the employee arrived to take up residence. An employee who moves into the destination home in May and whose household joins in June normally has a May completion date. An employee who attends a May training session, returns to the old residence and moves in June has a June completion date. The receiving manager supplies work dates if needed. The employee supplies the occupancy or travel evidence for the residence decision.

## REL-108.3 Delegation when the receiving manager is away

An acting manager can approve the receiving office budget if People Operations holds a written delegation covering that office, that type of spending and the date of approval. Forward the delegation with the request. A calendar invitation naming a vacation contact establishes whom to ask about office coverage. It does not grant that person budget authority. People Operations checks the delegation before releasing the authorization and records both the acting manager and the person whose authority was delegated.

If an employee is also the receiving cost-center manager, their manager signs the budget decision. An employee cannot approve their own transfer or an increase to their own category budget. Purchasing may collect quotes while signatures are pending. The carrier should receive the signed order only after People Operations releases the corresponding authorization. A tentative pickup slot marked subject to approval should carry no deposit or cancellation obligation.

## REL-108.4 Moving household goods alongside company equipment

During a site closure, Facilities may ship bench instruments, computers or office furniture on the same day as an employee's belongings. Inventory the business property on the Facilities manifest using asset tags. List household cartons on a separate inventory under the employee's transfer reference. Purchasing can ask one carrier to perform both jobs, but it must obtain an allocation showing which services belong to each order before accepting the quote.

If the final bill contains one unallocated line for a shared truck, Purchasing asks the carrier to reconcile it against the agreed allocation. The employee may submit separately purchased packing supplies while that reconciliation is open. Finance can hold the shipment line until the employee budget balance can be established. The employee should retain the original receipt even when the office has already scanned a copy for the closure project.

## REL-108.5 A postponed or withdrawn transfer

The receiving manager reports a postponement or cancellation to People Operations as soon as the office decision is made. The employee sends a list of existing reservations, refund terms, deposits already paid and orders that Purchasing placed directly. Purchasing cancels its own orders. The employee requests cancellation or credit on personal bookings. Record the time the vendor received the request and keep the vendor response. The cost of leaving a reservation active after the vendor offered free cancellation is personal.

For an employer-withdrawn transfer, Finance reviews unavoidable charges on bookings that were eligible and authorized when made. The manager provides the written withdrawal, and the employee supplies the original booking conditions and the final cancellation statement. A provisional card charge cannot establish the loss while a refund is pending. For a postponement, People Operations decides whether the existing authorization needs new dates or amounts before anyone rebooks. Treasury reconciles an outstanding advance against supported expenditures and issues instructions for any unused balance.

## REL-108.6 Reading the authorization history

The portal stores a sequence of dated events. An estimate is the requester's forecast. Budget approved means the receiving manager has accepted a proposed maximum. Released means People Operations has issued the written authorization to the employee. Paid means the named payee received funds. An event can appear on more than one system at different times. Use the signed version attached to the release event if a cached portal summary still shows an earlier amount.

An amendment applies to the fields it names. If it changes only the shipment ceiling, continue using the previously released lodging and travel amounts. A draft awaiting signature can be attached to the move file for discussion, but it cannot establish the budget for a purchase already made. For an audit, retain the original authorization, each released amendment and the purchase date. Those records allow Finance to reconstruct the amount in force when the employee made a commitment.

## REL-108.7 Resolving a move assigned to the wrong office

Sometimes the receiving manager works in a regional hub while the employee reports to a satellite office. Enter the actual permanent duty location in the destination field and the funding office in the cost-center field. Before release, People Operations compares the location in the transfer notice with the destination on the estimate. A budget owner in Tacoma can fund a move to Olympia. A Tacoma hotel in that example would require evidence that it qualifies as lodging at the authorized destination.

Send corrections under the existing transfer reference. Opening another request for the same person and route can create a second carrier reservation. If a duplicate has already been opened, People Operations designates the surviving reference, links the retired one and asks Purchasing and Treasury to move outstanding entries to the surviving record. Wait for confirmation before requesting a replacement advance or paying a second mover deposit.
