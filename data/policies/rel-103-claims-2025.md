# REL-103 · Filing a relocation claim

North Alder Instruments | Effective January 1, 2025

## REL-103.1 Preparing the claim

Open the relocation claim form in the employee expense portal. Enter the transfer reference from your written move authorization and the move completion date recorded by People Operations. This is the date you arrived at the destination to take up residence. If the date displayed in the portal differs from your records, contact People Operations for a correction. Use the authorization and any written amendments to identify the categories available for the transfer.

Use a separate expense line for each transaction and identify the vendor, transaction date, category, amount paid, and amount requested. If one transaction covers multiple categories, split it into separate lines and attach the same itemized record to each line. Identify any amounts billed directly to the company so Finance can reconcile the available budget. Exclude those amounts from the total requested for payment to you.

## REL-103.2 Receipts and supporting records

Attach the written move authorization, any amendments, and itemized receipts for the expenses you paid. A receipt must identify the vendor, date, items or services purchased, and amount. Where the invoice does not show payment, attach proof of payment as well. For a hotel stay, include the final folio with the room and tax charges by night. Show the portion you are claiming when a receipt includes personal purchases or excluded services.

If a receipt is lost, first ask the vendor for a duplicate. If the vendor cannot supply one, attach a signed statement identifying the expense and describing your attempt to obtain a duplicate. Include proof of payment and any available evidence of the service. Finance reviews the evidence and may decline an expense if the eligible portion cannot be established. A bank statement by itself may show what you paid without showing which items you bought.

## REL-103.3 Filing window

Use the claims edition in force on the recorded move completion date, including when the portal submission falls in the following year. Submit the claim within 60 calendar days after the move completion date. Count the day after completion as day one. The portal records the submission date when you select Submit and receive a confirmation number. Saving a draft or sending receipts to your manager leaves the claim unfiled. A weekend or company holiday counts as a calendar day. Keep the confirmation with your move records so you can verify the date if Finance asks about timing.

## REL-103.4 Review and corrections

Finance reviews the submitted lines against the authorization and the covered expense rules in REL-102. If a reviewer asks for more evidence, reopen the existing claim using its confirmation number, attach the requested record, and return the claim for review. The original submission date is retained for corrections to a timely submitted expense. Use the comments field to identify the line you changed so the reviewer can match the new evidence to the request.

Finance sends a decision through the portal showing the approved amount for each line. Read the explanation beside any reduced or declined amount before contacting the reviewer. For example, a hotel receipt may support the stay even when the nightly limit reduces the amount payable. If you later receive a vendor refund for a reimbursed item, notify Finance through the same claim and include the refund record so the payment can be reconciled.

## REL-103.5 Building an expense line from a final invoice

Use the final vendor invoice as the starting record. A quote describes an expected service. A booking confirmation identifies the reservation. A card notification shows that the card network authorized an amount. None of those records alone establishes what the vendor finally delivered and collected. For a prepaid service, include the prepayment receipt and the final statement applying that prepayment. Finance must be able to follow the payment from the employee to the eligible service.

The form has separate fields for amount paid and amount requested. Enter the full settled transaction in amount paid. Enter the eligible employee-paid portion in amount requested. Explain any difference in the line note. For a mixed transaction split between categories, allocate the paid amount once across the lines. The sum of the allocations should match the transaction total. Attach one copy of the receipt and associate it with each line using its attachment identifier.

## REL-103.6 Showing tax and an invoice-wide discount

When the merchant identifies tax by item, use the amount shown for the eligible items. When a single sales tax amount covers eligible and excluded items at the same rate, allocate the tax in proportion to the taxable line values after discounts. Retain cents through the calculation and round the final employee request to the nearest cent. If different rates apply and the receipt gives no breakdown, ask the merchant for the tax detail before estimating the eligible tax.

Suppose a store sells $80 of eligible cartons and a $20 personal cleaning kit. It discounts the entire taxable purchase by $10 and collects $7.20 in tax at a uniform rate. The cartons account for four fifths of the purchase, so allocate $8 of the discount and $5.76 of the tax to them. Enter $77.76 as the documented eligible amount before checking the shipment budget. Keep the complete receipt so a reviewer can reproduce the allocation.

## REL-103.7 Company cards, personal cards and shared receipts

The name printed on a reservation does not establish who paid. A centrally booked ticket may carry the employee's name while the booking agent invoices North Alder. Mark the base fare as company-paid. If the employee paid an eligible bag fee at the airport, create a separate employee-paid line for the bag and attach its receipt. State the ticket number in the note so Finance can associate the two records.

For a shared household payment, identify the purchaser, the household member who received the service and the authorization that covers the expense. A household member may give the employee the receipt for a covered service purchased on behalf of the move. The employee certifies that nobody else is seeking reimbursement for the same payment. When two transferring employees share a hotel folio, identify the designated reference for that stay and attach the folio there. Do not submit a second claim for half the room unless Finance has approved a documented split.

## REL-103.8 Electronic tolls and delayed billing

A toll account top-up adds money to an account that can be used on later journeys. To claim a toll, download the posted trip activity showing the road, date and actual charge for the approved route. Personal trips on the same statement may be redacted while leaving the eligible entries and the account holder visible. If a rental company bills a toll after issuing its final vehicle invoice, retain both documents and identify the trip on the claim.

If the vendor has not posted the charge when the employee prepares to file, identify the known expense, its service date and the pending itemization on a separate line before submitting. Finance holds that line for the actual posted amount. An open note about possible future expenses does not identify a transaction. A timely identified toll may be corrected when its record arrives. Filing a correction to another expense does not give an employee a new window for unrelated purchases.

## REL-103.9 Reading a returned claim

A returned claim retains its confirmation number. Open the reviewer message and note the specific line and requested evidence. A request for proof of payment usually means the invoice shows an amount due with no settled payment. A request for allocation usually means the invoice includes both covered and personal services. Upload the missing record to that line, explain what changed, and select Return to reviewer. Saving the attachment without returning the claim leaves it in the employee's queue.

A reviewer may approve the undisputed lines while a single charge remains open. The decision notice shows the status of each line and the amount released for payment. On resubmission, leave already approved lines in place. Starting a new claim for the entire move can place paid lines in duplicate review and delay reconciliation. If the original record is locked, contact Finance with the confirmation number and the line identifier so the reviewer can reopen the correct item.

## REL-103.10 What the portal status means

| Portal label | Event recorded | Record to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Employee saved an editable form | Draft transfer reference |
| Submitted | Portal accepted the filing | Submission confirmation |
| Returned | Reviewer requested a change | Reviewer message and line number |
| Approved | Finance authorized an amount | Line decision notice |
| Paid | Payment batch released | Remittance reference |

A status change can post overnight. If the employee selects Submit and the page closes, check the submission history for a confirmation before trying again. Two confirmations for the same expenses should be reported to Finance. Identify which record contains the complete filing and which was duplicated. An approval notice and a remittance notice may be issued on different days. Use the remittance reference when asking Treasury about a missing deposit.

## REL-103.11 Outage while filing

During a portal outage, record the time, error message and transfer reference. Contact the Finance service desk while the issue is occurring. Finance can accept the completed form and attachments through its designated secure intake channel and issue a manual submission confirmation. For this route, the filing date is the receipt date on that confirmation. A general service ticket that describes a login problem does not establish receipt of the completed claim. Contact the service desk again if the only response is a ticket number.

When the portal becomes available, Finance creates or links the portal record and copies in the manual receipt date. The employee checks that all attachments appear under the correct lines. A second submission can create a duplicate, so quote the manual confirmation in follow-up correspondence. Never attach bank account details or an unredacted full card number to a service ticket. Treasury uses the payment destination already on file.

## REL-103.12 Seeking review of a reduction

An employee may ask Finance for a second review within fifteen business days after the line decision notice. Identify the disputed line, the amount in question and the reason for requesting review. Attach the evidence or section supporting the request. If the issue concerns a posted company charge, Purchasing can supply the closed order and any vendor credits. If it concerns an approved category amount, People Operations supplies the released authorization history.

A different Finance reviewer compares the original decision with the evidence and sends a written determination under the same confirmation. The reviewer can correct an arithmetic error, an incorrectly attributed direct bill or a misread receipt. The reviewer cannot issue a retroactive spending authorization. A disagreement with the budget that was actually approved goes to People Operations for explanation. It does not turn a claim review into approval of a higher budget.

## REL-103.13 Refund after reimbursement

Record the vendor, original paid line, refund date, amount, and whether the refund was cash or a credit against another charge. A refund related entirely to an excluded service does not reduce the eligible service cost. Supply the vendor breakdown so Finance can make that determination. If the refund affects the entire transaction, Finance recalculates the eligible line after applying the discount or refund and compares it with the reimbursement already made.

For example, an employee paid $190 for one eligible room night and received $150 after the nightly cap. The hotel later returns $25 of the room charge. The revised cost still exceeds the cap, so the refund alone does not create an employee repayment. A $70 refund on the same facts reduces the cost to $120. In that case Finance calculates a $30 overpayment and Treasury issues the return instructions. Report either refund so the record agrees with the vendor.

## REL-103.14 Reconciling the remittance

Read the reimbursement advice from top to bottom. The first amount is the total of the employee-paid lines Finance allowed. Direct vendor payments appear in a separate informational field. An outstanding employee advance appears as a deduction. The remaining balance is the amount Treasury releases, subject to any previously released partial payments. A direct bill can reduce the budget available for an employee-paid line, but it is never a second payment due to the employee.

If the approved line amounts match the decision and the deposit differs, check for an advance or earlier partial payment under the same transfer reference. Contact Treasury with the remittance references if the difference remains. If the line amount itself is wrong, contact Finance under the original claim. Payroll questions about a statement entry go to Payroll with the transfer reference. The reimbursement advice documents the move settlement and does not calculate an individual's tax liability.
