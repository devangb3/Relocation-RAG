# REL-103 · Filing a relocation claim

North Alder Instruments | Effective January 1, 2025

## REL-103.1 Preparing the claim

Open the relocation claim form in the employee expense portal. Enter the transfer reference from your written move authorization and the move completion date recorded by People Operations. This is the date you arrived at the destination to take up residence. If the date displayed in the portal differs from your records, contact People Operations for a correction. Use the authorization and any written amendments to identify the categories available for the transfer.

Use a separate expense line for each transaction and identify the vendor, transaction date, category, amount paid, and amount requested. If one transaction covers multiple categories, split it into separate lines and attach the same itemized record to each line. Identify any amounts billed directly to the company so Finance can reconcile the available budget. Exclude those amounts from the total requested for payment to you.

## REL-103.2 Receipts and supporting records

Attach the written move authorization, any amendments, and itemized receipts for the expenses you paid. A receipt must identify the vendor, date, items or services purchased, and amount. Where the invoice does not show payment, attach proof of payment as well. For a hotel stay, include the final folio with the room and tax charges by night. Show the portion you are claiming when a receipt includes personal purchases or excluded services.

If a receipt is lost, first ask the vendor for a duplicate. If the vendor cannot supply one, attach a signed statement identifying the expense and describing your attempt to obtain a duplicate. Include proof of payment and any available evidence of the service. Finance reviews the evidence and may decline an expense if the eligible portion cannot be established. A bank statement by itself may show what you paid without showing which items you bought.

## REL-103.3 Filing window

Submit the claim within 60 calendar days after the move completion date. Count the day after completion as day one. The portal records the submission date when you select Submit and receive a confirmation number. Saving a draft or sending receipts to your manager leaves the claim unfiled. A weekend or company holiday counts as a calendar day. Keep the confirmation with your move records so you can verify the date if Finance asks about timing.

## REL-103.4 Review and corrections

Finance reviews the submitted lines against the authorization and the covered expense rules in REL-102. If a reviewer asks for more evidence, reopen the existing claim using its confirmation number, attach the requested record, and return the claim for review. The original submission date is retained for corrections to a timely submitted expense. Use the comments field to identify the line you changed so the reviewer can match the new evidence to the request.

Finance sends a decision through the portal showing the approved amount for each line. Read the explanation beside any reduced or declined amount before contacting the reviewer. For example, a hotel receipt may support the stay even when the nightly limit reduces the amount payable. If you later receive a vendor refund for a reimbursed item, notify Finance through the same claim and include the refund record so the payment can be reconciled.
